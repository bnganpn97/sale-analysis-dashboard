# Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
This project is an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze revenue, profit, product performance, and sales trends across multiple countries and regions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The dashboard provides business insights into:
- Revenue performance
- Profit margin analysis
- Product category performance
- Sales trends over time
- Best-selling products
- Regional business performance

---

## Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze profit margin across regions and product categories
- Identify top-performing products
- Track monthly sales trends
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualization

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Modeling
- Excel / CSV Dataset

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview
Provides a high-level business summary including:
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Revenue by Country
- Financial Performance by Year :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

### 2. Product Performance
Analyzes:
- Revenue distribution by country
- Profit margin by region and product category
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Product hierarchy and category performance :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

### 3. Detail Action Report
Detailed product-level analysis including:
- Revenue
- Profit Margin
- Profit
- Revenue Status
- Revenue MoM %
- Interactive filtering by time, region, and product category :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Key Insights
- The United States generated the highest revenue among all countries.
- Bikes contributed significantly to total sales performance.
- Several products achieved strong month-over-month revenue growth.
- Profit margins varied across product categories and regions.
- Monthly sales trends helped identify peak business periods. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
